# Song library

The worship team's song charts: uploaded to Google Drive by the team, then made searchable, transposable and printable as setlists on the site.

## Language

### Songs

**Song**:
A piece of music as the team knows it, e.g. *10,000 Reasons*, with its title, artist and original key.
_Avoid_: Track, piece

**Arrangement**:
One way the team plays a song. The first is the song's default arrangement; later ones are numbered (v2, v3…) unless given a label ("piano intro"), and are shown with their source ("v2 · YFM").
_Avoid_: Version (ambiguous with file format), description, variant

**Chart**:
The written-out music for an arrangement in one key: chords and lyrics the site can read, and the sheet it came from. An arrangement may have charts in several keys.
_Avoid_: Song sheet, score, file

**Same music**:
Two charts with the same lyrics and the same chords relative to their key, whatever their key or layout. Within one source, charts with the same music belong to one arrangement.
_Avoid_: Duplicate (say which: same music or same shape)

**Same shape**:
Two charts that look the same on the page (same pages, same lines in the same places), apart from their key. Arrangements from different sources merge only when their charts are the same shape, and then carry every source's tag ("YFM · ASE").

**As-is chart**:
A chart the site can't read (a picture, or outlines), so it's used exactly as uploaded and can't be transposed.
_Avoid_: Image chart, untransposable song

### Keys

**Chart key**:
The key a chart is written in, as uploaded. Taken to be the key the team usually plays it in, so the setlist builder preselects it.
_Avoid_: Default key, team key, uploaded key

**Original key**:
The key of the song's original recording.
_Avoid_: Recording key, source key

**Artist**:
Who recorded or wrote the song.
_Avoid_: Singer (ambiguous with a team member who leads it)

### Intake

**Source**:
A Google Drive folder, subfolders included, that the site takes charts from, with a short name: YFM, MM or ASE. People add songs only by uploading to a source; sources are listed in that fixed order.
_Avoid_: Drive folder, inbox, upload folder

**Correction**:
A fix to what the site worked out automatically (a title, an original key, a label, a wrong match, a file to leave out), made in Drive rather than on the site.
_Avoid_: Override, edit

### Setlists

**Setlist**:
Songs in order, each in a key, as built on Find songs and printed as one PDF.
_Avoid_: Playlist, song list

**Set**:
A setlist saved under the date of a service, for the whole team to see on Setlists. Worship leaders make them on the builder's **Service sets** tab. A date has at most one set.
_Avoid_: Service setlist, weekly set

**Share link**:
A setlist kept as a hidden snapshot, opened only through its link, made on the builder's **One-time** tab. It has no date and isn't a set; it's forgotten 30 days after it was last opened.
_Avoid_: Saved setlist, one-time set
